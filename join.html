What to send.

Email Dr. Nyelele with a short paragraph on the question you want to work on and why this lab is the place to do it, plus a CV. Naming a paper of ours that connects to your interests helps more than a long statement of purpose.

We read everything, and we try to reply to everyone. If a few weeks pass without a response, a follow-up is welcome rather than annoying.
