What to send

Email Dr. Nyelele with a short paragraph on the question you want to work on and why this lab is the place to do it, plus a CV. Naming one of our papers that connects to your interests helps far more than a long statement of purpose.

We read everything and try to reply to everyone. If a few weeks pass without a response, a follow-up is welcome rather than an imposition.
