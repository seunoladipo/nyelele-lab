About

Bridging the social and ecological sciences.

The Nyelele Lab is the research group led by Dr. Charity Nyelele in the Department of Environmental Sciences at the University of Virginia. Our work aims to improve understanding of issues at the nature–people interface: how nature delivers ecosystem services and benefits to people, and how to limit human impacts on the environment.

We are interested broadly in the links between biodiversity, climate change, and ecosystem services. The goal of our work is to inform locally tailored strategies and forest management efforts that address climate, environmental, and societal concerns together rather than one at a time.
