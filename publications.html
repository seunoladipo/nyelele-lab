Featured Ecohydrology 2024 Water Carbon

Valuation of forest-management and wildfire disturbance on water and carbon fluxes in mountain headwaters↗

Chung, M.G., Guo, H., Nyelele, C., Egoh, B.N., Goulden, M.L., Keske, C.M., et al.

[This study found that active forest management such as commercial thinning and low severity burnsreduces tree density and water consumption, boosting mountain water runoff for up to 15 years. Putting a concrete dollar value on these water gains gives land managers a practical financial roadmap to fund proactive treatments, helping protect overgrown forests from catastrophic wildfires and drought.]

2020
Urban Forestry & Urban Greening 2020 Environmental justice Heat

The equity of urban forest ecosystem services and benefits in the Bronx, NY↗

Nyelele, C., Kroll, C.N.

[This study found that urban tree cover and its associated ecosystem services such as heat index reduction and air pollutant removal are unequally distributed in the Bronx, with socioeconomically disadvantaged neighborhoods receiving significantly lower benefits. These findings matter because they highlight critical environmental inequities, demonstrating that municipal tree-planting and greening programs must strategically target marginalized, low-canopy areas to achieve environmental justice and improve public health.]
